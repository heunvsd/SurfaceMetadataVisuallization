Surface Metadata Visualization
Rubbing Metadata Visualization is a project about the translation of surfaces across media. It begins with physical rubbing, records each surface through metadata, and transforms the information into visual systems. Rather than seeing rubbing as a simple copy of texture, the project treats it as a record of contact: time, place, pressure, direction, paper, tool, and sensation. These records move from surface to image, from image to data, and from data into interactive web pages and printed posters.

Metadata Poster Archive
This page presents a series of posters generated from rubbing metadata. The original rubbing images are layered with ASCII textures created from spreadsheet-based records, turning collected information into visual surface. Each poster combines two metadata fields at a time, translating pressure, time, surface material, friction direction, and condition into different graphic systems. Through this process, each rubbing is read not only as an image, but as a record of contact.
